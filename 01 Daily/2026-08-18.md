* **Difficulty**: Medium | **Track**: Volume | **NeetCode #72**
* **Primary Pattern**: [[Backtracking]]
* **Focus**: Backtracking decision tree allowing element reuse. Branch 1: include `candidates[i]` and recurse on index `i` (same index). Branch 2: exclude `candidates[i]` and advance to index `i + 1`. Prune when `current_sum > target`.
* **Complexity Target**: $O(2^{T/M})$ time, $O(T/M)$ recursion stack space.
* **Platform Links**: [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/combination-sum/) | [NeetCode](https://neetcode.io/problems/combination-target-sum)

* **Complexity Target**: $O(N)$ time, $O(N)$ space.
* **Platform Links**: [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/problems/daily-temperatures/) | [NeetCode](https://neetcode.io/problems/daily-temperatures)
