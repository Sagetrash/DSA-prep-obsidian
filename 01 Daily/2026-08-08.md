## 💡 Key Learnings Today
* **Two Sum**: Single-pass Hash Map lookup (`diff = target - nums[i]`) achieves optimal $O(N)$ time & $O(N)$ space.
* **Group Anagrams**: Avoid `set()` for anagrams due to loss of duplicate character counts. Use `defaultdict(list)` with `key = "".join(sorted(i))` for in-place $\mathcal{O}(1)$ appends and clean `list(d.values())` output.
* **Contains Duplicate**: `len(set(nums)) < len(nums)` achieves $O(N)$ speed. Early-exit loop with `seen = set()` optimizes best-case time to $O(1)$.
* **Valid Anagram**: `sorted(s) == sorted(t)` or `Counter(s) == Counter(t)` quickly verifies multiset equality.
* **Top K Frequent Elements**: Bucket Sort pattern (array of lists indexed by frequency 0..N) achieves optimal $\mathcal{O}(N)$ linear time!
* **Product of Array Except Self**: Prefix & Postfix products multiplication `prefix[i] * postfix[i]` enables $O(N)$ division-free calculation with $O(1)$ extra space scalar accumulator.
* **Search Insert Position**: Binary search loop termination `while low <= high` naturally positions `low` at the exact insertion boundary.
