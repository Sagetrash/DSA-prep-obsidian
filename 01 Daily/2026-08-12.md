## 💡 Key Learnings Today
* **Stack LIFO Pattern**: `Valid Parentheses` (1-to-1 matching) and `Min Stack` (1-to-1 depth synchronization) demonstrate how LIFO structures eliminate searching.
* **In-Place Linked List Operations**: 3-pointer state transitions (`prev`, `curr`, `nxt`) reverse linked lists in $\mathcal{O}(1)$ space; dummy head nodes (`dummy = tail = ListNode()`) eliminate edge-case branches during list merging.
* **Floyd's Fast & Slow Pointers**: Moving `fast` by 2 steps and `slow` by 1 step detects cycles in $\mathcal{O}(N)$ time and $\mathcal{O}(1)$ space.
* **Tree Recursion**: Post-order DFS (`1 + max(depth(left), depth(right))`) and simultaneous DFS (`isSameTree`) cleanly solve tree depth and equality subproblems.
* **Queue Optimization in BFS**: Using `collections.deque.popleft()` achieves true $\mathcal{O}(1)$ pops per level, avoiding $\mathcal{O}(K)$ list shift overhead.

## ⚠️ Mistakes & Weaknesses Encountered
* **Queue Popping Overhead**: Used `list.pop(0)` ($\mathcal{O}(N^2)$) instead of `deque.popleft()` ($\mathcal{O}(N)$) on *Binary Tree Level Order Traversal*.
* **Subtree Scope Error**: Called `isSameTree(root.left, subRoot)` instead of `isSubtree(root.left, subRoot)` on *Subtree of Another Tree*, limiting search depth to 1 level below root.
