# 🚀 Long-Term Placement & Skill Engine Roadmap

This vault is built to serve as your **long-term personal DSA coach and placement command center** throughout your entire placement season and technical interview journey.

---

## 🎯 Multi-Phase Progression Blueprint

```text
Phase 1: 7-Day Sprint   ──►   Phase 2: 30-Day Engine   ──►   Phase 3: 90-Day Mastery
(35 Problems Solved)          (150 Problems Solved)          (300+ Problems Solved)

```

---

### 📍 Phase 1: 7-Day High-ROI Sprint (Current)
* **Goal**: Immediate placement round readiness & syntax refresh.
* **Target Pace**: 5–7 problems / day (2–3 High-Value Mediums + 3–4 Volume Easies).
* **Target Count**: **35 Solved** (Profile moves from 40 $\to$ 75+ Solved).
* **Focus**: Arrays & Hashing, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Binary Search, Stack, Linked Lists, Trees, Heaps, Basic DP.

---

### 📍 Phase 2: 30-Day Core Placement Engine (Weeks 2–4)
* **Goal**: Complete the NeetCode 150 & master all core Medium interview patterns.
* **Target Pace**: 4–5 problems / day (2 High-Value Mediums + 2–3 Volume Easies).
* **Target Count**: **150 Total Solved** (75 Mediums, 75 Easies).
* **Focus**:
  * Advanced Graph Algorithms (Topological Sort, Dijkstra's, Disjoint Set Union)
  * Advanced Tree Patterns (Trie, Segment Tree basics)
  * 2D Dynamic Programming (Knapsack, Longest Common Subsequence)
  * Monotonic Queue & Interval Scheduling

---

### 📍 Phase 3: 90-Day Engineering Mastery (Months 2–3)
* **Goal**: Top-tier tech company placement readiness (FAANG / Product-Based Companies).
* **Target Pace**: 3–4 problems / day (focusing on unseen Mediums/Hards & Timed Mock OAs).
* **Target Count**: **300+ Total Solved** (150+ Mediums, 30+ Hards).
* **Focus**:
  * Company-Specific Questions (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Uber tagged sets)
  * Weekly Timed Mock OAs under strict 60-min limits
  * Advanced Hard Problems & Systemic Edge-Case Mastery

## 🤖 Continuous AI Agent System Protocol

The AI agent will continuously manage your vault day after day:

1. **Daily Session Auto-Generation**: Simply say *"Build today's session"* or *"Give me today's problems"*, and the AI will auto-create the next note in `01 Daily/` with adapted problem recommendations.
2. **Automated Review Queue**: Overdue problems will return automatically to `05 Reviews/Revision Center.md` based on spaced repetition decay (1, 3, 7, 14 days).
3. **Adaptive Weakness Targeting**: The AI will analyze your mistake database (`04 Mistakes/`) and assign targeted reinforcement problems whenever a pattern weakness surfaces.
